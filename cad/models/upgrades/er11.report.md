# ER11 C8 collet chuck — high-fidelity model upgrade report

**Part:** C8-ER11A-100L straight-shank collet chuck (Ø8 mm shank × 100 mm,
ER11-A clamping nut) + ER11 collet
**Deliverables:** `er11_c8_hifi.py`, `er11_c8_hifi.step` (this directory)
**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Replaces (envelope):** `cad/cots.py::er11_chuck_c8` — 3 stacked cylinders
(nut Ø19 × 19, head Ø18 × 16, shank Ø8 × 100, Ø8.2 bore).

## 1. STEP search result — no legitimate no-login file found

A downloadable STEP was searched for first:

- **TraceParts / vendor CAD:** ER11 nut and C8-ER11A holder pages exist but
  gate CAD downloads behind account login.
- **GrabCAD / 3DContentCentral:** login-walled — skipped per task.
- **GitHub CNC projects:** hits are STLs / renders, no dimensioned STEP of a
  C8-ER11A-100L holder with an ER11-A nut under a redistributable license.
- **Rego-Fix / vendor sites:** publish **dimensioned drawings** (used below)
  but not free STEP of this exact holder.

Conclusion: no legitimate no-login STEP exists for this exact SKU, so a
**high-fidelity parametric build123d model** was built from cited drawings.

Notes on the ER11-A nut: it is the **standard "A" (Ausführung A) nut**,
OD **19 mm**, thread **M14 × 0.75** — distinct from the **ERM11 mini** nut
(OD 16 mm, M13 × 0.75). Sources give the nut height as 11.3–14 mm; **13 mm**
(the most common ER11-A "19 × 13" D×H figure, S4) was used. The real nut has
a light hex/spanner flat for tightening; the model captures the **stepped /
waisted Ø16→Ø19→Ø16 revolved profile** (the dominant collision envelope) and
omits the shallow wrench flats (they sit inside the Ø19 max envelope).

**Bounding box (measured):** X −9.50…+9.50, Y −9.50…+9.50, **Z −135.00…0.00**

### Drop-in frame contract (verified)
- axis **+Z**; nut **TOP face at z = 0.000** ✓
- shank extends **−Z**, Ø8 tip at **z = −135.000** (= −(35 + shank_len)) ✓
- Ø8 shank geometry **identical below z = −35** to the crude model → the two
  608ZZ spindle bearings that grip the shank seat unchanged ✓
- overall XY/Z bounding box **identical** to the crude envelope (Ø19 × 135) ✓

### Deliberate fidelity note — the bore is a seat, not a through-bore
The task says "Ø8.2 through-bore", but a Ø8.2 (r 4.1) bore run the full length
would be **larger than the Ø8.0 (r 4.0) shank and erase the shank entirely**.
The real holder has a **solid Ø8 shank**; the Ø8.2 is the **collet seat**. So,
exactly like the crude model (whose bore was only 26 mm long), the bore stops
in the head (z = +1 … −24) and the Ø8 shank stays solid. Measured bore Ø8.20.

## 4. CLEARANCE-CONSTRAINT FLAG CHECK

Validated envelope constraints (from `cad/params.py`):
`chuck_nut_r = 9.5` mm, `chuck_nut_len = 19.0` mm (nut + collet nose),
`grip_gap = 3.0` mm (stator bottom face → nut top face).

| Constraint | Envelope limit | Researched / measured | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nut outer radius** | ≤ **9.5 mm** | **9.500 mm** (Ø19 nut) | ⚠️ **AT LIMIT — ZERO MARGIN** |
| Nut + nose axial length below stator | ~**19 mm** | **13 mm** nut, nose flush at z=0 → 13 mm (16 mm incl. 3 mm grip_gap) | ✅ PASS (6 mm spare) |

### ⚠️ FLAG — radial: nut is EXACTLY at the 9.5 mm envelope limit
The real **ER11-A nut is Ø19 mm → outer radius 9.500 mm**, which **equals**
the validated `chuck_nut_r = 9.5` mm envelope exactly. It does **not exceed**
the limit, but there is **ZERO radial clearance margin**:

- Any ER11-A nut that runs to the high side of tolerance (some are listed at
  Ø19.x mm) **would exceed** the 9.5 mm envelope and break the validated
  clearance constraint for the M1 indexing sweep.
- If more radial margin is needed, an **ERM11 mini nut (Ø16 → r 8.0 mm)**
  gives 1.5 mm; note it needs an ERM-threaded (M13 × 0.75) chuck body.
- **Recommendation:** verify the outer diameter of the actual purchased nut.
  Many economy ER11-A nuts measure Ø18 mm (r 9.0, +0.5 mm margin); premium
  balanced nuts run a full Ø19.

### ✅ Axial: better than the crude envelope
The faithful ER11-A nut is **13 mm** tall, not the 19 mm the crude envelope
reserved. Axial space below the stator improves by ~6 mm; the `chuck_nut_len`
= 19 mm figure in `params.py` is **conservative** (safe, slightly loose).

## 5. How to regenerate

```
.venv/Scripts/python cad/models/upgrades/er11_c8_hifi.py
```
Exports `er11_c8_hifi.step` and prints the verification block in §3–§4.
`er11_chuck_c8(shank_len=100.0)` is the drop-in factory (matches the
`cots.er11_chuck_c8` signature and local frame).
